{%- comment -%} A pixel face, mood="happy" or "sad". Thirteen squares of 12 units a side, so drawn at 130px one square of the face is one square of the graph paper. site.js nudges it onto the lattice, turns eyes and mouth toward the pointer and makes it blink; heat.js colours it in as the pointer comes near. The sad one's cheeks show only when it is shy. {%- endcomment -%}
